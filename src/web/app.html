Hello

Meet Mitra, your calm companion between hospital visits.

MitraMind gives patients a safe place to talk about stress and worry, in English, Hinglish or हिन्दी, by text or by voice, and keeps their care team in the loop when it matters.

Hi! I'm Mitra.

Why MitraMind

Weeks between visits shouldn't mean carrying worry alone.

Hospital appointments are short and often weeks apart, and it can be hard to tell a doctor how you have really been feeling. MitraMind fills that gap: a friendly AI companion for the days in between, built with safety first.

Listens in your language

Type or speak in English, Hinglish or Hindi. Mitra replies with warmth and never diagnoses.

Safety comes first

If a message suggests someone may be in danger, Mitra shares helplines straight away and alerts the care team.

Your care team stays in the loop

Check-in scores, and only the messages you choose to share, help your next visit start with the full picture.
