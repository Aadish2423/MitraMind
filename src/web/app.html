Your privacy

Your chats are private. Your care team sees how often you use MitraMind, your questionnaire scores, and only the messages you choose to share. If something you write suggests you may be in danger, your care team is alerted so they can help.

MitraMind is a support tool, not a doctor and not for emergencies. In a crisis call Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24x7) or 112.
